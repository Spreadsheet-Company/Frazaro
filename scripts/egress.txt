The egress fixture (SEC.15). Each case sits under a line "=== <name>" and
runs to the next such line; these lines before the first header are
ignored. A case's lines are one text as the formula sink receives it
(what "Put formula ... into cell ..." hands to VlaSetFormula, with its =
when it has one), joined with a line break where there are more than
one. VlaWriteEgressGolden (VLA_Tests.bas) writes VlaFormulaEgress'
(VLA_Runtime.bas) reading of every case to egress_golden.txt: one line
"REFUSED<TAB><the function named, or DDE for a DDE link>" where the sink
refuses to write the text, or "WRITTEN" where it writes it. The core's
egress.rs is held to that file by its own test, and
tools/check_egress_golden.ps1 holds the pair's shape, the two lists equal
and every listed name reached. A case is named for what it means to
reach; the golden records what the reference reads.
