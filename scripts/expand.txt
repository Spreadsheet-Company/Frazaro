The expand fixture (VLA.3). Each case sits under a line "=== <name>" and
runs to the next such line; these lines before the first header are
ignored. A case's lines are one VLA text, joined with a line break where
there are more than one, expanded to its fixpoint under the prelude as
VlaExpandText (VLA.bas) expands it. VlaWriteExpandGolden (VLA_Tests.bas)
writes every case's record to expand_golden.txt: "EXPANDED<TAB><the macro
applications that fired>" and the text VlaExpandText prints, or
"REFUSED<TAB><the catalogue's id><TAB><the refusal's text>". vla-lang is
held to that file by tests/expand_golden.rs, and
tools/check_expand_golden.ps1 holds the pair's shape and every one of the
twenty-seven expand-time primitives reached. A case is named for what it
means to reach; the golden records what the reference does. No case
raises a refusal that names a line, so an edit of the prelude moves
nothing here.
