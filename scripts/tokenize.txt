The token fixture (PORT.6, slice 6b). Each program sits under a line
"=== <name>" and runs to the next such line; these lines before the first
header are ignored. VlaWriteTokenGolden (VLA_Tests.bas) writes EnTokenize's
reading of every program to tokenize_golden.txt, one token per line as
"<line><TAB><token>", or one REFUSED line where the tokenizer refuses; the
core's tokenizer (core/src/english/tokenize.rs) is held to that file by its
own test. A program's text is exactly its lines: a blank line inside one is
a paragraph marker on purpose, so keep the sections tight.
